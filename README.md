# VGE Solar Panneling rebalance

Rebalances the Solar Panneling from VGE to be more affordable as early game option.
Changes:
HP: 50-> 200 (Mostly to make them not die immediately to a stiff breeze)
Cost: 10 Gravlite + 1 Component -> 2 Gravlite + 1 Component
Subscaffold moved from Standard to Basic Gravtech. This is so Solar panels have the early game advantage over Astrofuel Generators (that they do not cut into the Tile Budget). 

Background: 
In VGE as is, Solar Panneling produces 200W/Tile when sun shines. This is an almost 100% increase over vanilla solar panels (106W/Tile) but I found myself to never build them. The Problem is that there is better energy/tile and cost/W available with Astrofuel Generators. Unless you play on a Planet free of Vegetation it is almost always more efficient to build those (400W/Tile) as you produce Astrofuel already and each 1600W generator needs a 70 Chemfuel > 35 Astrofuel craft every 11.6 days. It also costs 100 Steel and 3 Components. 

So the convenience of "never refuel" does not really compare to the high cost of panneling at 10 Gravlite a tile and the inability to really produce the tiles until the final research (and even then its 2.5 Crafts of 4xGravlite). If you lost one it was even more painful as they die very easily and you lose 5 Panels/tile. With a cost of 2 per tile you will most likely be limited by your components, and its still only 200W/Tile/Sun but the advantage of them being able to build on Subscaffold is always available. This allows early ships with a Solar "dish" around them. The extra HP also helps with keeping them alive longer so you have to replace them less often.
