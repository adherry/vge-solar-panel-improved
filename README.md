# VGE Solar Panneling rebalance

Rebalances the Solar Panneling from VGE to be more affordable as early game option.
Changes:
HP: 50-> 150 (To reduce mortality against stray bullets and lightning)
Cost: 10 Gravlite + 1 Component -> 2 Gravlite + 1 Component
Subscaffold moved from Standard to Basic Gravtech. This is so Solar panels have the early game advantage over Astrofuel Generators (that they do not cut into the Tile Budget). 

Background: 
In VGE as is, Solar Panneling produces 200W/Tile when sun shines. This is an almost 100% increase over vanilla solar panels (106W/Tile) but I found myself to never build them. The Problem is that there is better energy/tile and cost/W available with Astrofuel Generators. Unless you play on a Planet free of Vegetation it is almost always more efficient to build those (400W/Tile) as you produce Astrofuel already and each 1600W generator needs a 70 Chemfuel > 35 Astrofuel craft every 11.6 days. It also only costs 100 Steel and 3 Components. 

So the convenience of "never refuel" does not really compare to the high cost of panneling at 10 Gravlite a tile and the inability to really produce the tiles until the final research (and even then its 2.5 Crafts of 4xGravlite). A lost panel also loses you 5 Gravlite. With the cost reduction having some of them die is not as big a loss any more.

Note: to use the Pill you need some extra mods:
Mandatory:
- To make sure you have Walls: [Hull Style: NANAME Walls](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3543791340) 
- For the Fuel Tanks: [Floor Mounted fuel tanks](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625783623) Only for starting with this ship as without you lack fuel tanks.

Optional:
- Storage: [sbz Gravship storage](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3537905298) Without this mod the 2 Storage containers will disappear but 2 Shelves remain.
- Coolers: [Replace Stuff - Continued](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3526354009) Without you have no coolers preinstalled.
- The Gravjumper Computers (more science): [GravTech - Big Cannons](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3628174105) Without you get less Grav Research but its the most optional mod for the ship.
- Steel Heatsinks + Oxygen Tank: [Gravship Disbalance Expanded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3743054625) Without you have to rebuild a bit to include a heatsink and you won't have oxygen.
